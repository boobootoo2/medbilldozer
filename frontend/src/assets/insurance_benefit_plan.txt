Synthetic Insurance Benefit Plan Summary (Demo Use Only)

Plan Overview
This document is synthetic and provided for demonstration purposes only. It represents a typical commercial PPO-style health insurance plan.

Deductibles & Cost Sharing
• Annual deductible: $2,000 (individual)
• Coinsurance: 20% after deductible
• Out-of-pocket maximum: $6,500

Covered Services
• Preventive screening colonoscopy: Covered at 100% when billed as screening
• Diagnostic colonoscopy: Subject to deductible and coinsurance
• Imaging (CT/MRI): Covered with medical necessity documentation
• Chemotherapy infusion: Covered with prior authorization

Exclusions & Limitations
• Duplicate or unbundled services are not reimbursed
• Incompatible procedure combinations may be denied
• Excessive units require clinical justification

Member Responsibilities
• Verify accuracy of bills and EOBs
• Request itemization when charges are unclear
• Appeal denied or questionable claims within 180 days
